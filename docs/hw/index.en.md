1. First complete the whole assignment yourself, then compare against the "approach";
2. For questions you got wrong, go back to the corresponding chapter (Chapters 1/2/3/5/6);
3. For programming questions, do not memorize the payload; focus on understanding the **stack layout** and **offset computation**.
